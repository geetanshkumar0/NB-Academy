1 · How fires start

Fire needs three things: heat, fuel and oxygen. Remove any one and the fire goes out.

At work, the usual causes are overloaded sockets, damaged cables and rubbish stored near heat sources.

2 · Raise the alarm

If you discover a fire, break the nearest alarm point and call the emergency number. Only tackle a small fire if you are trained and have a clear exit behind you.

3 · Choose the right extinguisher

  • Water: paper, wood, fabric. Never on electrics.
  • CO₂: electrical equipment.
  • Foam: flammable liquids.
  • Wet chemical: cooking oil fires.

4 · Evacuate safely

Leave by the nearest safe exit. Never use a lift. Close doors behind you and go to the assembly point. Do not go back in until a fire marshal says it is safe.
